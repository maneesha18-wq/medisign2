About MediSign

Revolutionizing healthcare communication through AI

🎯 Our Mission

MediSign is dedicated to breaking down communication barriers in healthcare for deaf and hard of hearing individuals. Using cutting-edge artificial intelligence and deep learning, we've built a system that instantly recognizes medical sign language and converts it to text and speech.

Our goal is to make healthcare more inclusive and accessible by providing real-time translation of medical sign language, empowering medical professionals and patients to communicate effectively.

🔧 How It Works

MediSign uses a sophisticated deep learning pipeline to analyze medical sign language videos:

  1. Video Processing: The system extracts 60 frames from your video at consistent intervals
  2. Feature Extraction: MobileNetV2, a pre-trained neural network, converts each frame into a 1280-dimensional feature vector
  3. Sequence Modeling: A BiLSTM model with attention mechanism analyzes the temporal sequence of movements
  4. Recognition: The model predicts which of 10 medical terms was signed
  5. Output: Results are displayed with confidence scores and audio playback
